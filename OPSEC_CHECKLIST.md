# OPSEC Review Checklist

**Purpose:** Before any page, document, or image is published on the ARSC USMC Field Reference, it must pass this checklist. Log the review by noting the reviewer, date, and result below or in the page footer.

**Authority:** CDR Brian S. Ford, MC, USN (maintainer). If in doubt, route through 1st MLG PAO or Security before publication.

---

## Checklist

- [ ] **No classified content.** Nothing that has ever been classified, or that a reasonable person would consider likely to be classified.
- [ ] **No CUI / FOUO content.** No material marked Controlled Unclassified Information, For Official Use Only, or equivalent.
- [ ] **No personally identifying information.** No names, ranks, SSNs, service numbers, home addresses, or other PII of specific individuals (other than CDR Ford's published authorship).
- [ ] **No specific unit locations.** No present or future location of 1st Med Bn, 1st MLG, or subordinate units beyond what has already appeared in open press.
- [ ] **No planned operations or named exercise specifics.** No information about upcoming operations, exercises, or deployments not already in open press.
- [ ] **No AMAL line-item NSN data.** No National Stock Numbers for specific line items beyond what appears in publicly released NAVMED publications.
- [ ] **No SOPs not approved for public release.** Command SOPs go through explicit approval before posting to `public/standards/`.
- [ ] **Every doctrinal claim has a citation.** All clinical, operational, and organizational claims link to a publicly released source.
- [ ] **No reproduction of copyrighted material beyond fair use.** Short attributed quotations are acceptable; reproducing CPG text in bulk is not.
- [ ] **If in doubt, route to 1st MLG PAO or Security.** When any item is uncertain, hold publication until cleared.

## How to use this checklist

1. Run the checklist for every new or substantially edited page before merging to `main`.
2. For Standards PDFs, run the checklist before placing the PDF in `public/standards/`.
3. Log the review in the table above (or in a per-page footer note when appropriate).
4. If any item fails, hold the content and resolve or escalate before publishing.
